## Definitions

ROC = net P&L / (fill x 10 + entry fee), mean over trades; CI = game bootstrap, 2,000 reps, numpy default_rng(20261004). Win rate = mean payout (ties 0.5). Break-even win rate = mean (fill + fee / 10). Sharpe x365 = daily net P&L with zeros on non-trade season days, mean / sd x sqrt(365). Daily Sharpe = mean / sd of daily net P&L on game days only. Max DD and worst day on daily net P&L; skew of daily net P&L on game days. Excl top 5 = without the 5 trades with the largest net P&L on that line. Costs x2 = fill + 1 more cent (capped at 0.99, trade kept) and fees x2. Full detail: training_results.csv, training_trades.csv.
