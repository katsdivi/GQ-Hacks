\section{HYPOTHESIS\_v4: the live hypothesis}\label{app:v4}

HYPOTHESIS\_v4 is the project's live hypothesis. It is untested: no confirmatory result exists yet. It was committed to \texttt{main} as \texttt{HYPOTHESIS\_v4.md} in \texttt{94aa1a0} at 07:56:29 ET on 2026-10-04, before any of its test data was examined. Its Arms A and B are two settings of one passive maker; they are unrelated to the v3 strategies A and B (main note, section ``Before the plot twist''). The frozen text, verbatim:

\begin{quote}\small
HYPOTHESIS\_v4 (post-hoc origin, frozen before test data is examined).\\
Origin: posthoc-mm-signal N maker, polymarket.com, Oct 3 games kicking off by 20:00 ET (863eb69, f535256): +1.09 c per contract at +60 s [0.17, 2.12], concentrated in 5 games, matched round trips +0.52 c.\\
Rule: passive maker quoting both sides at the best bid and ask (join, never improve), 10 contracts, inventory cap +/- 50, no Kalshi signal. Arm A: always quote. Arm B: quote only while the spread is >= 3 c.\\
Venues: polymarket.com (recorded books and trades); Polymarket US (authenticated order-book websocket with venue timestamps, recorded live; test set 2 only).\\
Fills: strict trade-through only. Maker fee: each venue's published schedule at the time (cite). Kickoff cut [T - 2 min, T + 20 min].\\
Primary metric: P\&L per contract marked to mid at +60 s, game-bootstrap 95\% CI (2,000 reps, seed 20261011). Secondary: matched round-trip P\&L; held to settlement; excluding the top 5 games.\\
Test set 1 (run once now, descriptive only, small n): Oct 3 games kicking off after 20:00 ET with complete polymarket.com books and trades on disk, never used by posthoc-mm-signal.\\
Test set 2 (confirmatory): all college and NFL games from 2026-10-08 00:00 ET to 2026-10-13 00:00 ET, run once after the window closes.\\
Decision (test set 2, per venue and arm, Holm across venues): supported only if (1) primary CI above zero, (2) point estimate above zero excluding the top 5 games, (3) settlement P\&L point estimate above zero. Otherwise not supported. Report either way.
\end{quote}

\textbf{Status.} Test set 1 was not run: 0 eligible games, because every late Oct 3 recording ends at 00:34 ET, before those games ended (\texttt{posthoc-mm-v4} \texttt{1d3d68a}). Test set 2 (Oct 8 to 13) needs live recording of polymarket.com books and trades and the Polymarket US order book with venue timestamps; that recorder is not yet running. The sealed Aug to Oct 2 holdout cannot test v4: for that period the repository holds only polymarket.com taker trades at 1 s resolution and no order books, so the join and trade-through fill rule cannot be simulated.

\textbf{Where v4 came from (descriptive, one day, not pre-registered).} A simulation on recorded Oct 3 polymarket.com top-of-book data (88 games, 428 fills, maker fee 0, queue position unknown) earned \$46.53 marked to mid at +60 s and \$114.03 held to settlement (\texttt{posthoc-mm-signal} \texttt{b346230}, \texttt{863eb69}). Excluding the top 5 games it is +0.15 c per contract [$-0.48$, +0.77], and 57.6\% of the 59 games with fills were positive (38.6\% of all 88). Spread captured was +\$63.18 against $-\$15.65$ of adverse selection. Under the reading rule applied to the 59 games with fills, all four conditions pass, so the team reads it as a lead worth testing live (\texttt{863eb69}); the rule was written after these numbers were seen, and read over all 88 games (38.6\% positive) its game-share condition fails, which is why the live test decides. The 88 games are holdout-period games (Oct 3 and 4); no committed file records an authorization to use them, and we disclose it here.


\section{Sharpe ratios: method}\label{app:sharpe}

For the v3 strategies, \texttt{report\_book.py} sums net P\&L per ET calendar day over the 60 holdout days (2026-08-06 to 2026-10-04), keeping zero days, divides by a fixed capital base (which does not change the Sharpe), and reports mean over standard deviation times $\sqrt{365}$, with a risk-free rate of 0; the NW5 version uses a Newey-West 5-lag standard deviation. We recomputed these from the committed trade files (\texttt{results/holdout/}, \texttt{main} \texttt{f4f3e28}) and they match \texttt{numbers.json}. 95\% intervals from a 5-day block bootstrap (10,000 draws), v3 strategies only (not v4): v3 Strategy A $-3.81$ [$-6.05$, $-0.50$], v3 Strategy B $-7.76$ [$-9.95$, $-6.07$], v3 combined $-7.78$ [$-10.13$, $-5.97$]. Daily skew is about $-3.2$ to $-3.7$, so the bootstrap intervals are the ones to quote. For v4's origin, only one day exists, so no daily or annualized Sharpe is possible; the per-game Sharpe (mean over standard deviation of game P\&L, 88 games, game bootstrap) is +0.22 [+0.06, +0.35] at +60 s and +0.16 [$-0.04$, +0.35] held to settlement.
